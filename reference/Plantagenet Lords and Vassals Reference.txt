Vassals
Regular Vassals
Each Vassal counter shows a coat of arms, the Vassal's Seat (on the back), and one or two ratings:

Loyalty — left numeral, colored red (Lancastrian) or white (Yorkist). Added to the Levying Lord's Influence rating if the color matches the Levying side, subtracted if it opposes. Some Vassals have no Loyalty rating.
Service — right numeral. Sets how far right on the Calendar the Vassal's second counter is placed when Mustered (determines Pay/Disband timing).

Names and Seats below are from the map file. Loyalty and Service ratings to be filled in.

Special Vassals
Single counters with light blue (Yorkist) or pink (Lancastrian) backgrounds, no Seat, no Loyalty, no Service ratings. Mustered via a Capability card rather than Levy Vassal action; do not set up on the map and do not aid Tax; do not appear on the Calendar; need not be Paid. If the Special Vassal Disbands, discard the Capability card that placed them.
Hastings

Side: Yorkist
Capability: Y24 Hastings
Eligible Lord(s): Edward IV
Effect: Levied free; also adds 2 Men-at-Arms units and Command +1 to Edward IV. Command +1 ends if Hastings Disbands. The 2 Men-at-Arms are not affected by Hastings Disbanding (but per FAQ #6, they ARE removed during Waste).
