Plantagenet Strongholds Reference
 
Note on Tides of War wording: For regular Strongholds (City, Town, Fortress), the side with the MOST total Favour markers of that type earns the Influence points (one award per type, regardless of how many Strongholds of that type the side holds). For Special Strongholds (London, Calais, Harlech), the Influence points are awarded INDIVIDUALLY to whichever side has Favour at that specific Stronghold. The rules wording in 4.8.1 reflects this distinction.
 
================================================================
REGULAR STRONGHOLDS
================================================================
 
CITY
  Levy Troops:   1 Longbow + 1 Militia
  Supply:        2 Provender
  Tax:           2 Coin
  Pillage:       2 Provender + 2 Coin
  Tides of War:  +2 Influence for most Favour
 
TOWN
  Levy Troops:   2 Militia
  Supply:        1 Provender
  Tax:           1 Coin
  Pillage:       1 Provender + 1 Coin
  Tides of War:  +1 Influence for most Favour
 
FORTRESS
  Levy Troops:   1 Men-at-Arms + 1 Longbow
  Supply:        1 Provender
  Tax:           1 Coin
  Pillage:       1 Provender + 1 Coin
  Tides of War:  +1 Influence for most Favour
