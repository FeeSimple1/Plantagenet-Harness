================================================================
FAQ / CLARIFICATIONS
================================================================
 
1) Sail action does not allow to go directly from a port to
   another port on another sea. Neither Great Ships, which
   affects only Parley, Tax and Supply.
 
2) The Lord's Retinue and Vassals also count as forces during
   the Sail action.
 
3) Exiled lords can only be mustered in their Exile box during
   the Muster Exiles phase.
 
4) Fallen Brother Capability, Clarence removal through the
   Foreign Haven rule also counts.
 
5) Campaign game: If a scenario is Tied (IP at 0 at end of
   scenario), victory goes to the King.
 
6) Capability Hastings additional men-at-arms are removed
   during Waste.
