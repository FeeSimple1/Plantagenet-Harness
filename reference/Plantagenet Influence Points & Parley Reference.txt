Plantagenet Influence Points & Parley/Pass Reference
(Transcribed from the player aid foldout)
 
================================================================
PARLEY
================================================================
  - At or adjacent to target Stronghold (including by Sea).
  - Target Locale: no Enemy Lord.
  - If Lord at target, add or remove Favour (shift 1 level toward
    Friendly).
  - If adjacent, check Influence (1.4.2) to do so (3.4.1, 4.6.4).
 
================================================================
PASS
================================================================
  - None. Do nothing (4.6.5).
 
* Commands require 1 action, unless "all".
 
Sharing (1.5.3): Any Assets among Lords at same Locale.
Greed (1.7.2): Discard Assets or cards only as required OR to move.
 
================================================================
INFLUENCE POINTS (1.4.1)
================================================================
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
SPEND
----------------------------------------------------------------
 
Influence Check (1.4.2) for Parley, Levy, or Tax:
  -1 (always)
  -1 to raise Rating +1
  -3 to raise Rating +2
  -1 per Way (Parley)
  -1 if by Sea (Parley)
 
Levy - Pay Lords (3.2):
  -1 per Lord at Stronghold
  -2 per Lord in Exile box
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
LOSE
----------------------------------------------------------------
 
Unpaid/Unfed:
  If Troops Pillage:
    -2x Assets taken
  If Lord Disbands:
    -Influence Rating
    -1 per Vassal (3.2.1, 4.7, 4.8.2)
 
Exile (4.3.5):
  -Influence Rating
  -1 per Vassal
 
War Setup (6.3 IIIY, IIIL):
  -8 per Heir removed
 
----------------------------------------------------------------
GAIN
----------------------------------------------------------------
 
Battle Won (4.4.3):
  +Influence Rating of losing Lords
  +1 per losing Vassal
 
Arts of War (1.9.1):
  +/-? by card text
 
Tides of War (4.8.1):
  +1 Lord in each North/South/Wales
  +2 Dominate each North/South/Wales
  +2 Favour at London    (no Enemy Lord)
  +2 Favour at Calais    (no Enemy Lord)
  +1 Favour at Harlech   (no Enemy Lord)
    NOTE: The "no Enemy Lord" condition applies to ALL THREE
    Special Strongholds (London, Calais, and Harlech). On the
    foldout, a single bracket groups all three with that
    qualifier.
  +2 Most Favour at Cities
  +1 Most Favour at Towns
  +1 Most Favour at Fortresses
  +? by Capability card text (1.9.1)
  If "Gain Lords Influence" Turn:
    +Influence Ratings of on-map Lords
