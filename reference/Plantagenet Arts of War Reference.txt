================================================================
PLANTAGENET — ARTS OF WAR CARD REFERENCE
================================================================
Source: Background Book pages 31–51 (Arts of War – Notes), with
Errata & Clarifications applied. History sections removed.

Each Arts of War card has an EVENT (top half) and a CAPABILITY
(bottom half). Only one section is in effect at a time. Italicized
subtitle text on cards is historical flavor and has no effect on
play (1.9.1) and is omitted here.

Reminder of italicized card type tags:
- "Hold": keep hidden, play later when conditions allow
- "This Levy" / "This Campaign": effect lasts to end of that phase;
  the card's Capability is unavailable that turn (it is not
  discarded until end of Levy / end of Campaign).
- Otherwise: reveal and resolve, return to deck.

================================================================
YORKIST EVENTS  (top half of card)
================================================================

---- ALL SCENARIOS ----

Y1. LEEWARD BATTLE LINE
Hold: Play in Battle to halve all Missile hits (round up) to
Friendly Lords, unless Enemy Leeward Battle Line.
Tips: Play at Event step after Battle Array (4.4.1). First add any
Missile Hits from other cards, then halve the total (round up). If
both sides play this Event, neither has effect. Attacker may wait
to see if Defender plays before deciding.

Y2. FLANK ATTACK
Hold: Play to Intercept without rolling and become Attacker in
that Battle.
Tips: Play when attempting Intercept (4.3.4), including by Marshal
or Lieutenant. Cannot be played while Y12/L20 Parliament's Truce
is in effect. Intercept succeeds automatically; otherwise occurs
normally.

Y3 & Y9. ESCAPE SHIP
Hold: Play upon Death check if Yorkist Route (4.5.1) to Port for
any Routed Yorkists to go into Exile instead.
Tips: Play before rolling for Death (4.4.3); playing side must be
at, or trace overland Route to, a Friendly Port (4.5.1). Defender
must decide and play any Death-check Events before Attacker.
Selected Routed Lords automatically apply Exile (4.3.5) instead of
the Death roll. Works even if Blocked Ford was played that
Battle. Combines with Y8 England Is My Home: result is regular
Disband instead of Exile.

Y4. JACK CADE
This Levy Yorkist Lords in or adjacent to Yorkist Dominated Areas
each add 2 free successful Parleys.
Tips: At this turn's Muster (3.4), determine which Areas Yorkists
Dominate (Favour at all Locales in the Area, 1.3.1, 4.8.1). All
Yorkist Lords in those Areas, or in Locales adjacent (by Way) to a
Locale within such an Area, must take two Parley Levy actions
(3.4.1) — without spending Influence or Lordship and succeeding
without rolling. Capability not available this Turn (3.1.3).

Y5. SUSPICION
Hold: Play in Battle for participating Yorkist Lord to check
Influence. Success Disbands 1 Lancastrian Lord at Battle with
lower Influence rating.
Tips: Play at Event step after Battle Array (4.4.1). Choose one
Friendly Lord and one Enemy Lord. The Friendly Lord must have a
higher PRINTED Influence rating than the Enemy [ERRATA: ignore
any modifications by other cards for this comparison]. Friendly
Lord checks Influence (1.4.2). On success, Disband the Enemy Lord
(3.2.4) — no Influence-point loss. Disbanded Lord does not take
part in the Battle; Reposition in Round 1 (4.4.2).

Y6. SEAMANSHIP
This Campaign Yorkists may Sail for just 1 Command action.
Tips: Friendly Lords may Sail by usual rules (4.6.1) for one
Command action instead of an entire Command card. May combine
Sail with other actions on the same card (March, Tax, Supply,
Parley, more Sailing). Capability not available this Turn (3.1.3).

Y7. YORKISTS BLOCK PARLIAMENT
This Levy Lancastrians may not Levy Vassals except by Event.
Tips: Blocks the Levy Vassal action this turn, including via
Capability such as L32 Two Roses, and for Special Vassals such as
L35 Thomas Stanley. Lancastrian Events that enable Vassal Levy
(L7 For trust not him, L35 Margaret Beaufort, L37 The Earl of
Richmond) supersede this card and allow Vassal Levy. Capability
not available this Turn (3.1.3).

Y8. EXILE PACT
This Campaign Yorkist Lords may use a Command action to place
their cylinder into a Friendly Exile box (at no Influence cost).
Tips: Allows voluntary Yorkist Exile without enemy Approach. Costs
1 Command action; Active Yorkist Lord goes directly to a
scenario-designated Exile box, no effect on Influence, Assets, or
Vassals.

Y10. TAX COLLECTORS
Each Yorkist Lord may immediately conduct a Tax action to receive
twice the usual Coin.
Tips: Optional. Any on-map Yorkist Lord may Tax regardless of
location, but must comply with the usual Tax procedure (4.6.3) —
Influence check, selecting/tracing to/Depleting a qualifying
Stronghold — except Coin obtained is doubled.

Y11. BLOCKED FORD
Hold: Play upon Approach to prevent Exile (all Lords there Battle).
Tips: Play at the moment of Approach by either side (including
from Intercept, 4.3.4). Forbids any Lords there from choosing
Exile before Battle (4.3.5). Does NOT affect Escape Ship. When
L15 King's Parley cancels an Approach, Yorkists cannot play
Blocked Ford. Lancastrian play of Blocked Ford prevents use of
Y8 England Is My Home.

Y12. PARLIAMENT'S TRUCE
Hold: Play to prohibit all Approach and Intercept for the rest of
this Campaign.
Tips: Play at any moment during a Campaign. Forbids both sides'
Approach and Intercept for the rest of the turn, including by
Y2/L2 Flank Attack or by L29 High Admiral. Return to deck on
Reset (4.8.6).

Y13. ASPIELLES
Hold: Play to inspect all Lancastrian Held cards and 1 Hidden
Lancastrian Lord mat (1.5.3).
Tips: Play at any moment, even mid-Battle. Under Hidden mats
option (1.5.3), select any one Enemy Lord's mat to inspect.

---- SCENARIOS Ia, Ib, & Ic ----

Y14. RICHARD OF YORK
This Levy Yorkist Influence ratings +1 for Parley.
Tips: +1 affects all Yorkist Lords (not just York's), for Parley
Levy (3.4.1) only — not for Levying Lords, Vassals, or Troops.
Capability not available this Turn. Card discarded at end of
Levy; no effect on Campaign Parley (4.6.4).

Y15. LONDON FOR YORK
If Yorkist Favour at London, add a second Favour there. London
changes Favour only by Event or Pillage.
Tips: If London has one Yorkist Favour marker, add a second. If
already two, no effect. Double Favour means London is immune to
Lancastrian Parley actions (3.4.1, 4.6.4) unless enabled or aided
by Events such as L17 My crown is in my heart or L18
Parliament Votes. Otherwise treat London as Yorkist Favour
normally (e.g., Tides of War, 4.8.1). Pillage (3.2.1) can remove
or reverse Favour at London normally, removing both Yorkist
markers.

Y16. THE COMMONS
This Levy each Yorkist Levy Troops action may add up to 2 Militia
extra (from pool).
Tips: Each time a Yorkist Lord takes Levy Troops this Levy, may
add up to 2 Militia in addition to other Troops, so long as
Militia remain in pool (1.6). Capability not available this Turn.
Discarded at end of Levy; Militia remain.

Y17. SHE-WOLF OF FRANCE
Shift each Yorkist Vassal's Calendar marker 1 box right.
Tips: Identify Yorkist Vassal markers on the Calendar; shift each
one Calendar box right (delays Pay or Disband by one turn,
3.2.3). Status of Lancastrian Lord Margaret does not affect this
Event.

Y18. SUCCESSION
This Levy 1 Parley action by each Yorkist Lord costs less by –1
Influence point and automatically succeeds.
Tips: Each Yorkist Parley action this Levy phase only costs 1
Influence less (min zero) and succeeds without rolling. Does NOT
affect Parley Levy actions already reduced to zero by other
cards. Capability not available this Turn.

Y19. CALTROPS
Hold: Play in Battle to add +2 Melee Hits against the Enemy each
Round.
Tips: Play after Battle Array (4.4.1). Each Melee step of each
Round, +2 Hits against Lancastrians. Yorkist player distributes
between Engagements; Lancastrians among Lords/Forces within an
Engagement.

Y20. YORKIST PARADE
Hold: Play if London Friendly and York or Warwick there. This
Levy Yorkist Influence ratings +2.
Tips: Play any time London Favours Yorkists. If played during
Campaign, takes effect during the FOLLOWING turn's Levy; the
card's Capability not available until thereafter (3.1.3).
Increases all Yorkist Influence ratings +2 during that Levy. Does
NOT affect Tides of War "Gain Lords Influence" (4.8.1).

Y21. SIR RICHARD LEIGH
Remove Lancastrian Favour from London or, if neutral, place
Yorkist Favour there.
Tips: Occurs even if a Lancastrian Lord is present at London.

Y22. LOYALTY AND TRUST
This Levy any 1 Yorkist Lord has Lordship +3.
Tips: Select any one Yorkist Lord. +3 is in addition to other
Event/Capability modifiers. Capability not available this Turn.

this card when played; Capability immediately available for Levy

Y25. OWAIN GLYNDWR
This Campaign no Lancastrian March or Sail to any Strongholds in
Wales.
Tips: Takes precedence over any other Event/Capability enabling
March or Sail. Forbids March or Sail INTO any Stronghold in Wales
even for Lancastrian Lords already in Wales. Capability not
available this Turn.

Y26. DUBIOUS CLARENCE
If Edward IV on map, he may check Influence to Disband Clarence.
Lords: Edward IV and Clarence.
Tips: Effect requires both Edward IV and Clarence cylinders at
Strongholds or Exile boxes. Influence check at Yorkist's option.
On success, Disband Clarence (3.2.4) — no Influence-point penalty.

Y27. YORKIST NORTH
Gain 1 Influence point for each Yorkist Stronghold and each
Yorkist Lord in the North.
Tips: Immediately add 1 Yorkist Influence per Stronghold in the
North Area (1.3.1) with Yorkist Favour, plus 1 per Yorkist Lord
at a Stronghold within the North. Even if Influence then exceeds
Victory Threshold (5.2), play continues; Victory Check happens
at end of Campaign phase.

Y28. GLOUCESTER AS HEIR
This Levy Gloucester may Parley +3 times for 0 Lordship.
Lord: Gloucester or Richard III.
Tips: At Yorkist option, Gloucester (or Richard III in full-length
scenario) if on map at the outset of Muster (3.4) takes up to 3
Parley actions that do not spend Lordship (in addition to any with
his Lordship). Otherwise follow Parley Levy rules (3.4.1),
including Influence cost and rolls. Capability not available
this Turn.

Y29. DORSET
This Campaign Devon at Exeter Parleys for no Influence cost and
automatic success.
Lord: Devon.
Tips: Does NOT affect Levy-phase Parley (3.4.1). Affects only
Devon's Campaign Parley actions while at the City of Exeter. His
Parley Commands from there cost 0 Influence and auto-succeed,
otherwise follow rules (4.6.4) — costs Command actions and remain
limited to Exeter and adjacent Strongholds Wells, Dorchester,
Launceston, Plymouth, extended to other English Channel Ports if
Stafford has or Shares a Ship. Capability not available this Turn.

Y30. REGROUP
Hold: Play in Battle for a Lord once to roll each of his Routed
Troops' (modified) Protection for them to recover.
Tips: Play after Battle Array (4.4.1). At any one moment of the
Battle, choose a Lord. All that Lord's Routed Troops (wooden unit
pieces, NOT Retinue or Vassals) roll Protection. On success,
slide them out of the Routed section — no longer Routed (can Rout
again normally). On failure, no effect; remain Routed.

Y31. EARL RIVERS
Add up to 2 Militia to each Yorkist Lord on map.
Tips: Optional; may include Yorkist Lords in Exile boxes (but NOT
on Calendar). Limited by Militia available in pool (1.6).

Y32. THE KING'S NAME
This Levy Gloucester (not Richard III) may cancel each successful
Lancastrian Levy action by paying 1 Influence point.
Lord: Gloucester.
Tips: After any successful Lancastrian Levy Action (3.4.1–3.4.6) —
Parley by Levy action, Levy Lord, Levy Troops, Levy Vassal, Levy
Transport, or Levy Capability — Yorkist may pay 1 Influence to
cancel the action's result. Lancastrian Influence costs incurred
remain spent, but Depletion/Exhaustion incurred is reversed.
Capability not available this Turn.

Y33. EDWARD V
This Levy Gloucester (not Richard III) has +3 Lordship.
Lord: Gloucester.
Tips: +3 Lordship in addition to other modifiers. Capability not
available this Turn.

Y34. AN HONEST TALE SPEEDS BEST BEING PLAINLY TOLD

Parley costs +1 extra Influence Point.
Tips: From the moment this Event occurs to end of turn, each
Lancastrian Parley costs 1 more Influence than otherwise. Even
actions that would have cost zero Influence (e.g., Parley Command
targeting Lord's location 3.6.4, or Parley using Events such as
L17 My crown is in my heart) now cost 1 Influence. Capability
not available this Turn.

Y35. PRIVY COUNCIL
This Levy all Yorkist Influence ratings +1.
Tips: +1 to all Yorkist Lords' Influence from the moment played to
end of current Levy phase. Capability not available this Turn.

Y36. SWIFT MANEUVER
Hold: Play in Battle. Whenever a Lancastrian Retinue Routs, if
desired, immediately end the Round.
Tips: Play after Battle Array (4.4.1). May end any Round at the
moment a Lancastrian Retinue (NOT Vassal or Troops unit) Routs;
go directly to Lord Rout step (4.4.2). Skips remaining Hits, more
Engagements, etc.

Y37. PATRICK DE LA MOTE
Hold: Play in Battle for Yorkist Culverins and Falconets to add 2
dice of Hits (not just 1 die).
Tips: Play after Battle Array (4.4.1). Y1 and Y2 Culverins and
Falconets in that Battle add 2 extra dice of Missile Hits in
Round 1 instead of 1 die.


================================================================
YORKIST CAPABILITIES  (bottom half of card)
================================================================

---- ALL SCENARIOS ----

Y1 & Y2. CULVERINS AND FALCONETS
At start of Round 1 of Battle, this Lord may discard this card to
add 1 die roll of Missile Hits.
Lords: Any.
Tips: After both sides decide on Events and before Round 1,
Defender then Attacker decide whether to expend Culverins and
Falconets. Use only on Round 1; rolls a die at that side's
Missile Strike step adding 1–6 extra Missile Hits, then discard.

Y3. MUSTER'D MY SOLDIERS
Each Battle at a Friendly Stronghold, this Lord adds 2 Men-at-Arms
and 1 Longbowmen. Remove them after Battle.
Lords: Any.
Tips: At outset of Battle Array (4.4.1) at a Friendly Stronghold,
add 2 MAA and 1 Longbow to that Lord's mat. On Aftermath
(4.4.4), remove those Troops (only, not the card). If Lord left
without Troops, he Disbands (1.6).

Y4. WE DONE DEEDS OF CHARITY
Each Tides of War, this Lord may pay 1 or 2 Provender (may Share)
for +1 Influence point each.
Lords: Any.
Tips: Once during each Tides of War (4.8.1), discard 1 Provender
(may Share, 1.5.3) for +1 Influence, or 2 Provender for +2.

Y5. THOMAS BOURCHIER
This Lord starts in any Friendly City Command +1.
Lords: Any.
Tips: "Command +1" = a Lord with Command rating 2 instead takes 3
Command actions (1.5.2, 4.2.1). Applies only if this Lord, at
START of his Command Card, is at a Friendly CITY (not Town,
Fortress, Special Locale such as London, or Exile box). Bonus
applies for entire Command card, even if he later moves away.

Y6. GREAT SHIPS
This Lord's Ships count double for Sail and Supply and connect
all Ports of all Seas.
Lords: Any.
Tips: This Lord's Ships (including when Shared TO others, but NOT
others' Ships shared TO this Lord) carry double on Sail: 12
Forces, 4 Provender, 4 Carts (4.6.1). On Supply from a Port, add
2 Provender per Ship (4.5.1). Renders all Port Strongholds and
Exile boxes as 1 Way distance for PARLEY, SUPPLY, and TAX — as
if all on the same Sea (1.3.1 Seas And Ports). This Lord Levies
Ships normally (3.4.5).
[FAQ #1]: SAIL action does NOT allow going directly from a port to
another port on a different sea, even with Great Ships. Great
Ships affects only Parley, Tax and Supply for cross-Sea linkage.

Y7. HARBINGERS
This Lord's Supply actions draw twice the usual Provender.
Lords: Any.
Tips: For any Supply by this Lord, calculate normal Provender
added (4.5.2), place double. Mandatory. Cumulative with cards
that affect Supply (e.g., Y6 Great Ships). Does NOT affect
Forage (4.6.2).

Y8. ENGLAND IS MY HOME
When going into Exile, this Lord instead Disbands to the next
Calendar box.
Lords: Any.
Tips: Whenever this Lord would go into Exile (including via Y3/Y9
Escape Ship), instead Disbands (3.2.4) — cylinder placed
automatically into next Turn's Calendar box (no roll for
position). No Influence-point penalty. Blocked Ford prevents
use of England Is My Home.

Y9. BARRICADES
At Friendly Strongholds, this Lord's Men-at-Arms have Armour 1-4
and Longbowmen and Militia have Armour 1-2.
Lords: Any.
Tips: Applies regardless of who is Attacker, whenever the
Stronghold where this Lord Battles is Friendly. Longbowmen and
Militia become Armoured Troops. Modification does NOT apply when
rolling for Losses (4.4.3).

Y10. AGITATORS
This Lord can use a Command action to Deplete an adjacent Neutral
or Enemy Stronghold or flip Depleted there to Exhausted.
Lords: Any.
Tips: 1 Command action each; can Deplete/Exhaust multiple
qualifying Strongholds on same Command card. Combinable with
March, etc. Adds no Assets/Troops. Lancastrian Lords' presence
does not affect this.

Y11. YORKISTS NEVER WAIT
This Lord Marches alone (not in a Group) on Road as if Highway.
Lords: Any.
Tips: 1 March action moves 2 Ways if each is Highway or Road
(4.3.3). Marshal/Lieutenant with other Lords must leave them
behind on Road to use this. Does NOT affect Path March.

Y12. SOLDIERS OF FORTUNE
When Levying Troops, this Lord may pay 1 Coin to also add 2
Mercenaries (from pool).
Lords: Any.
Tips: Only way Mercenary units enter play. On Levy Troops (3.4.4),
spend 1 Coin (may Share, 1.7) to add 2 Mercenary units in
addition to normal Troops. Must meet usual Levy Troops
requirements/costs. Receive 0/1/2 Mercenaries depending on what
remains in pool (1.6). Card stays with Lord until Disband, even
when no Mercs remain.

Y13. SCOURERS
This Lord's Forage actions always add +1 extra Provender.
Lords: Any.
Tips: Each Forage adds +1 Provender, even if unsuccessful (which
would yield 1 Provender instead of 0 and still NOT Deplete the
Locale). Mandatory. Does NOT affect Supply (4.5).

Y14. BURGUNDIANS
This Lord at any Port adds 2 Handgunners (once, maximum).
Lords: York or March.
Tips: Y14/Y23 Burgundians is the only way Handgunners enter
play. The first time only that this Lord is at any Port
Stronghold (not Exile box, regardless of Favour) — including upon

Disband, even if Handgunners are Lost (no replacement).

Y15. NAVAL BLOCKADE
This Lord at a Port cancels Lancastrian actions using Ports on
that Sea unless a roll of 1-2.
Lord: Warwick.
Tips: Whenever Warwick (with this Capability) is at any Port

immediately after any Lancastrian action using a Port on the same

include Parley, Levy Ship, Supply, Sail, and Tax (3.4.1, 4.6.4,
3.4.5, 4.5, 4.6.1, 4.6.3). If action requires an Influence check
(1.4.2), Lancastrians may wait to see roll before paying
Influence and rolling check. Lordship/Command cost is spent
regardless. Does NOT impede use of a Stronghold itself that is
also a Port, nor card effects requiring a Port (Heralds, French
Troops).

the full 5 (or what remains in pool, 1.6). Card stays with
Warwick until Disband.

Y17. ALICE MONTAGU

Reduces Domination requirement from "all Strongholds Friendly" to
